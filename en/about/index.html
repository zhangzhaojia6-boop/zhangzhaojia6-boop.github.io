About Xintai

Founded in 2001 in Huiguo, Gongyi: an aluminum sheet, strip and foil producer running from raw material to fabrication.

Henan Xintai Aluminum Industry Co., Ltd was founded in 2001 in the Gongyi industrial zone, Henan. Registered capital is 120 million yuan, the site covers 780,000 ㎡ and the company employs over 2000 people.

The plant runs 9 cold rolling lines, 41 casting mills, one 1850 mm double-coiler hot mill and a 260 m coil-coating line for heavy gauge, plus 2 automatic spray lines, 3 colour-coating lines and over a hundred CNC brakes, shears, routers and turret punches.

6000 t of 1xxx, 3xxx, 5xxx and 6xxx curtain-wall substrate is held in stock. Perforated, carved and double-curved panels are fabricated at 3000 ㎡ a day and 100,000 ㎡ a month.

Xintai panels are used on projects for Wanda, Jianye, Vanke and Greenland, and on airports, high-speed rail stations, metro lines and hospitals across China. The company is listed in the Xiong'an New Area building material catalogue.
